Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy new locks?

Usually not if your existing locks are compatible and in working condition. Damaged or unsuitable hardware may need replacement.

Can you rekey several locks to one key?

Often, if the lock cylinders and keyways are compatible. We confirm before work begins.

How much does rekeying cost?

Pricing depends on lock type, number of cylinders, travel and the condition of the hardware. Call for an exact quote.
