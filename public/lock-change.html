Frequently asked questions

Should I change my locks or rekey them?

Rekeying changes the working key on compatible existing hardware. Replacement makes sense for worn locks, damage or an upgraded style or function.

Do you bring the new locks?

Yes. We can discuss and supply compatible lock hardware or review a lock you already purchased.

Can you install a smart lock?

Yes, subject to door and hardware compatibility. Ask about keypad and Wi-Fi setup with installation.
